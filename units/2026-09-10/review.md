# CSC110: Data types, collections and variables — review and guidance

Unofficial student study notes. Consult official course materials for current requirements. Practice questions are not official assessments.

## Revision checklist

**Explicit assessment signals**
- Numeric result-type questions were identified as typical quiz/test practice. Track intermediate types, especially with `/`, `//`, and mixed numeric operands.
- Cross-type equality was identified as a worksheet issue: equal values can collapse into one set element despite having different types.

**Selected study priorities—not additional test predictions**
- Distinguish literals, expressions, assignment statements, and equality comparisons.
- Know what `in` checks: substrings in strings, elements in lists/sets, and keys in dictionaries.
- Use zero-based and negative indexing correctly; sets have no positional indexing.
- Choose lists for order or repeated entries, sets for distinct unordered elements, and dictionaries for key–value lookup.
- Recognize `set()`, `[]`, and `{}` as different empty collections.
- Trace assignments in order. Reassigning a name does not recalculate values previously assigned to other names.
- Distinguish out-of-range indexing, missing dictionary keys, undefined names, and invalid assignment targets.
